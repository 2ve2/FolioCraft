# Ichnara editorial identity review

Date: 2026-10-18. Proposed creative direction under the human's authorization to develop and show professional work. This is not human acceptance or a deployed brand replacement.

## Construction

- Wordmark: STIX Two Text SemiBold with explicit optical spacing for I/c, c/h, h/n, n/a, a/r and r/a; outlined into SVG paths. It is an identified existing typeface, not a claimed custom font. The STIX Open Font License is included as OFL.txt.
- Symbol: a separately drawn capital I with asymmetric cut terminals and two open framing corners. The frame suggests presenting an individual's work; this is a design interpretation, not established Ichnara etymology.
- Small icon: a simplified I without the surrounding frame on a violet rounded square, avoiding thin framing elements at small sizes.
- Palette: aubergine #291D38, violet #6840C7, lavender #E9E0F3 and paper #F7F4EE. Existing application interface fonts remain unchanged.
